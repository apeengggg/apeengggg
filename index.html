You Suffer But Why?

1.316seconds. That's all you get.

Blink and it's over. That's the whole point.

Four words. No chorus.

Napalm Death recorded it at Rich Bitch Studio in Birmingham in 1986 and put it on Scum the next year. It runs for 1.316 seconds. The band thought of it as a joke to play live, and it still turned into one of the most talked-about songs in heavy music.

The joke works because the question is real. It doesn't blame the world, the system or the Monday morning. It points back at you: you suffer — but why? It comes with no answer, only one more second to think about it, and then the song is over.

You can't explain it the way you'd explain a long song. You live it, and then you ask yourself again.
